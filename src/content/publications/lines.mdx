## Research Question

How do polling-place operations, staffing, time of day, and voter ID requirements relate to waiting and voting times?

## Main Finding

Lines and waiting times varied with time of day, with mornings busiest. Photo ID requirements had different associations with check-in times in majority-white and majority-nonwhite polling places.

## Research Design

A multi-site observational study of polling-place operations during the 2016 presidential election.

## Data Employed

Research teams directly observed and timed voters at polling places in 28 jurisdictions, recording queues, check-in times, ballot completion times, and departures from lines.

## Substantive Importance

The study documents how polling-place operations and election rules relate to the time voters spend casting their ballots.
