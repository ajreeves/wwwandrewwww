Forthcoming in *Handbook on American Political Geography*, edited by Nicholas F. Jacobs and Kal Munis. The chapter combines county-level electoral distributions with Cooperative Election Study evidence to show that nationalization standardizes partisan competition without erasing geographically rooted political differences.
